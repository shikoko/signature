Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy full signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings. Stay on the General tab.
  3. Scroll to Signature, click Create new, name it Full, click inside the box and paste (Ctrl+V, or ⌘V on a Mac). Delete your old signature here if you like.
  4. Do the same with Copy short signature and name it Reply.
  5. Under Signature defaults, set For new emails use to Full and On reply/forward use to Reply.
  6. Tick Insert signature before quoted text in replies and remove the "--" line that precedes it, so your reply signature sits under your message, as in the preview.
  7. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Why it's built this way

Profile links are words by default. Outlook and many company mail servers block images, so logo icons turn into empty boxes or show up as attachments. Words always appear and are easier to tap on a phone.

Bright brand colors are often too light for small text. Android green (#3DDC84) measures 1.8:1 against white, and small text needs about 4.5:1 to be easy to read. The contrast check under the preview tells you when a color is too light.

Everything uses tables and inline styles in Helvetica or Arial, because Gmail removes stylesheets and web fonts from signatures.
